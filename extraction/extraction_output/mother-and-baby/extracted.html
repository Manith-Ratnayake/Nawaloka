Frequently Asked Questions

SEO-Optimized FAQs

1. Why is Nawaloka recommended for maternal health in Sri Lanka?

Nawaloka offers complete maternal health services including pregnancy, safe deliveries, cesarean surgery, postnatal wellness, and NICU support, all managed by experienced specialists in Colombo.

2. Do you provide cesarean surgery (C-section) in Colombo?

Yes. We perform both planned and emergency cesarean surgeries in fully equipped operation theatres with 24/7 specialist availability.

3. Is your maternity hospital suitable for high-risk pregnancies?

Absolutely. Our team specializes in managing high-risk pregnancies with advanced monitoring, NICU support, and emergency obstetric treatment.

4. Do you offer newborn intensive care (NICU) services?

Yes. Our NICU is equipped to treat for premature babies, low birth-weight infants, and newborns requiring specialized medical attention.

5. How can I book an appointment at Nawaloka?

You can book an appointment by calling our maternity centre, submitting an online enquiry, or visiting our Colombo hospital directly.
