Legacy of Quality

Certifications & Landmark Achievements

Long-standing recognitions that reflect Nawaloka Hospitals PLC’s commitment to quality, safety and service excellence.

1

ISO 9001:2015

Nawaloka Hospitals PLC has established a quality management system in accordance with ISO 9001:2015 international standard. The quality management system encompasses all functional areas and follows naturally from customer-related functions through operations to delivery or discharge, meeting the specified requirements of customers. Nawaloka Hospitals PLC was the first ISO certified private hospital in Sri Lanka in the year 2000 and has retained the standard for over twenty years.

2

5 Crown Award for Food Hygiene

Nawaloka Hospitals PLC received recognition for the quality and hygiene of its food through the prestigious 5 Crown Award for Food Hygiene by Ind-Expo Certification Ltd. The hospital first achieved this award in 2012 and continued to strengthen its food storage and preparation practices over the years.

3

National Quality Award

The Sri Lanka National Quality Award is presented annually to recognise Sri Lankan organisations that excel in quality management and quality achievement. The programme is organised and implemented by the Marketing and Promotion Division of Sri Lanka Standards Institution. Nawaloka Hospitals PLC was the only hospital to win the Sri Lankan National Quality Award in 1998.

4

National Business Excellence Award

Awarded by the National Chamber of Commerce, this recognition honours business enterprises that demonstrate excellence while contributing to the economic progress of the country. Nawaloka Hospitals PLC and its laboratory services continue to work towards total customer satisfaction through efficient, accurate, safe and quality-driven healthcare services.
