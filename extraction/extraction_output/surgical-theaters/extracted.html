Where Surgical Precision Meets Compassionate Treatment

At Nawaloka Hospitals Colombo 02, our surgical theatres are a cornerstone of our commitment to clinical excellence. Whether you're preparing for a planned procedure or facing an emergency, you can trust that your surgery will be performed in one of Sri Lanka’s most advanced operating theatres, supported by highly skilled teams who prioritize your safety and wellbeing.

A World-Class Surgical Environment

Our modern surgical complex is equipped with cutting-edge technology, precision monitoring systems, and state-of-the-art sterilization protocols that meet global standards. Each operating room is designed to reduce infection risk, support complex procedures, and provide surgeons with the tools they need for optimal outcomes.

From minimally invasive laparoscopic surgeries to high-precision neurosurgical and cardiac procedures, Nawaloka’s surgical theatres are ready for it all - 24/7.
