<h6>Our Core Physiotherapy Services</h6>
<span>✅ Pain Management (Joint &amp; Muscle)</span>
<p>Say goodbye to chronic pain. Our joint and muscle physiotherapy specialists offer targeted therapy to reduce inflammation, increase flexibility, and eliminate discomfort — restoring your freedom of movement.</p>
<span>✅ Neurological Rehabilitation</span>
<p>Regain independence with specialized rehab programs for stroke, spinal injuries, Parkinson’s disease, and more. Improve balance, coordination, and strength under expert neurological guidance.</p>
<span>✅ Arthritis Rehabilitation</span>
<p>Find relief from osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis through gentle, effective physiotherapy. Reduce stiffness and pain while improving joint mobility and daily functioning.</p>
<span>✅ Orthopaedic Pre/Post-Operative Wellness</span>
<p>Optimize surgical outcomes with pre- and post-operative orthopaedic physiotherapy. From joint replacements to spinal surgery, our therapies support quicker healing and stronger recovery.</p>
<span>✅ Musculoskeletal Therapy</span>
<p>Our musculoskeletal physiotherapy addresses back pain, neck pain, postural issues, and repetitive strain injuries with evidence-based, functional movement therapy.</p>
<span>✅ Sports Injury Rehabilitation</span>
<p>Get back in the game with advanced wellness for sprains, ligament tears, overuse injuries, and fractures. Our sports therapists help athletes recover faster and reduce risk of re-injury.</p>
<span>✅ Pediatric Physiotherapy</span>
<p>Supporting children with cerebral palsy, Down syndrome, and developmental delays, our child-friendly therapy sessions focus on improving motor skills and mobility in a safe environment.</p>
<span>✅ Geriatric Physiotherapy</span>
<p>Empowering seniors to stay active and independent. We address balance disorders, arthritis, falls, and post-hospital rehab for elderly patients with compassionate, specialized treatment.</p>
